## Design rules
- Minimal, premium, intuitive; avoid obvious labels, redundant instructions and decorative controls.
- Gold, white and silver on dark backgrounds; preserve existing public site design.
- Mobile-first; visually verify spacing, readability and controls on iPhone before acceptance.
- Portfolio: drag directly on image, tap for fullscreen, no image labels; first image is cover. Small trash icon centered at bottom; hover on desktop, visible on touch. Visible controls 28px with 44px hit areas. Fullscreen gold arrows and trash; after deletion show neighboring image.
- Moderation: three decisions, no checklist. Multiple addresses per master.
- Keep approved v15 unchanged; create a new version for further work.
